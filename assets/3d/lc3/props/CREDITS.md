# Prop mesh sources

`add_site_props.py` imports these meshes and repaints them with the model's own materials, so their
original materials and colours are not used. The files are kept as downloaded.

- `park-bench-j-toastie.glb`: "Park Bench" by J-Toastie, from Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza/m/kn0sMwbspb),
  licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
  498 triangles. Scaled to 1.44 m and then by the script's PROP_SCALE (0.55), and recoloured;
  geometry otherwise unchanged.
- `water-cooler-j-toastie.glb`: "Water Cooler" by J-Toastie, from Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza/m/zWi9p5FSxD),
  licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
  1,566 triangles. Scaled to 1.4 m and then by PROP_SCALE (0.55), and recoloured; geometry otherwise
  unchanged.
